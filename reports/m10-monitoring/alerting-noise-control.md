# M10 alerting, deduplication and noise control

## Incident lifecycle

Incident identity is `(check_id, scope, subject_key, dedupe_generation)`. Only one open incident may exist per stable check and subject. Transient observations update counters and last-seen evidence; they do not emit a new alert each time.

Recommended transition rules:

1. **Candidate:** a single bad observation creates/updates a candidate result, no notification.
2. **Open:** open after the signal-specific consecutive-failure/time-window threshold. Record first failure, confirmation time, reason, source, and sanitized evidence.
3. **Ongoing:** update `last_observed_at`, count, and severity; send no duplicate message. Escalate once only when severity changes or a material duration threshold is crossed.
4. **Resolve:** require recovery confirmation, close the same incident, record recovery time/duration, and send one resolution message if an opening message was successfully delivered or policy requires it.
5. **Flapping:** if at least 3 open/resolve transitions occur in 30 minutes, retain observations but suppress repeated messages for 30 minutes; send a single flapping summary and one stable recovery notice.

Defaults are starting values and must be validated in staging with synthetic targets:

| Check | Open threshold | Recovery threshold | Repeat/escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website HTTP | 3 failed probes of 5-minute cadence within 15 minutes, with a second DNS/TLS/network classification sample | 2 successful probes at least 2 minutes apart | no repeat for 30 minutes; one escalation after 30 minutes if still critical |
| TLS | one confirmed invalid/expired result; expiry <=7 days critical, <=14 days warning, <=30 days info | valid chain on next independent daily check; expired certificate requires verified recovery | notify on threshold crossing only, not daily repetition |
| Connector | use existing Stale/Disconnected thresholds; require a second due evaluation before notification for `Stale`, immediate transition when explicit revoked/incompatible | 2 fresh heartbeats/polls or restored current presence | no repeated messages; escalation once after 2 hours disconnected |
| Backup | consume terminal failed state immediately; RPO overrun when allowed age is exceeded; stuck only beyond engine timeout plus grace | next verified backup in policy window | one per backup/RPO incident; no alert per transfer retry |
| Queue/scheduler | threshold persisted for 2 samples; scheduler stale after 5 minutes from its 1-minute heartbeat; queue age >15 min critical only after 2 samples | 2 healthy samples | group by queue/worker; one repeat after 30 minutes |
| Shared provider | 2 failed safe probes or 3 correlated normal-operation failures across separate operations | 2 successful observations | provider incident suppresses duplicate downstream site alerts where failure is clearly shared |

## Dedupe and correlation

Use a database uniqueness constraint or transaction-safe open-incident claim, plus idempotent outbox keys `(incident_id, transition_sequence, channel, recipient_id)`. A scheduled overlap lock alone is insufficient for delivery uniqueness. Use a row lock/atomic unique insert under MySQL concurrency; test concurrent check and delivery workers.

Correlation may group impact under a shared provider/region signal while retaining child site observations. Correlation does not erase site evidence or claim causality without evidence. If the source is ambiguous (DNS, S3, app egress), report independent reasons and mark causal relationship suspected.

## Maintenance and suppression

- Monitoring continues during declared maintenance; only delivery is suppressed.
- Windows have scope (one site, deployment, provider check), owner, start, end, creation reason, and hard maximum duration (recommended 24 hours for sites, 2 hours for deployment unless explicitly extended with audit). Expired windows close automatically and cannot silently extend.
- Deployment suppression may be generated by the existing deployment pause/maintenance marker; never trust a client header/query parameter.
- Suppression is attached to the incident transition and visible in the console. A critical security signal and a monitoring-pipeline/backup encryption failure should bypass ordinary quiet hours, but still honor a declared incident freeze only with Owner acknowledgement.
- Quiet hours are deferred for v1. Global infrastructure incidents must be visible even while email is suppressed.

## Recipients and channels

Initial policy: infrastructure/DR/queue/provider incidents go to Owner only. Customer-site critical availability/Connector/backup incidents appear in Owner and permitted Operator consoles; email goes to Owner and assigned Operator only. Customers receive no M10 operational email until a customer-facing policy and templates are approved. Security notices continue through the existing security workflow. No Slack/Teams/Discord/PagerDuty.

In-app incident state is authoritative. Email is an idempotent delivery adapter through the existing queued mailer. Store queued/sent/failed timestamps and a safe failure category; use bounded retries and surface exhausted delivery as a separate monitoring-system incident. Email failure must not re-open the site incident or block its resolution.

## Acknowledgement and audit

V1 acknowledgement means “seen/owned by operator”, not resolution and not suppression. Record actor, incident ID, timestamp and optional short note in the existing audit model. Only authorized Operator can acknowledge a site incident; Owner can acknowledge platform-wide. Acknowledgement does not change health or close the incident. Reassignment/escalation trees are deferred.

## Data safety

Notification subject/body contains reason, safe website label/domain, incident ID, first/last observed UTC, duration and a link to authorized detail. Never include credentials, APP_KEY, Connector bearer values, DB/object URLs with signed query parameters, raw payloads, archive names that reveal private paths, security exploit content, stack traces, or customer content. Redact domain paths/query; use safe host only.

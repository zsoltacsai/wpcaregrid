# Purpose and lawful-basis engineering matrix

This is not a legal determination. “Candidate” is a question for counsel and the actual relationship/contract, not an approved basis. GDPR Article 6 categories are listed as review prompts only. The organization must document purpose, necessity, balancing/consent requirements, notice, retention and role before enabling processing. See official [GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679) and EDPB [controller/processor guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-072020-on-the-concepts-of-controller-and-processor-in-the-gdpr_en).

### Required approval record

For each approved purpose retain: purpose statement; role; category and fields; Article 6 basis and, if relevant, Article 9 condition; necessity and balancing/consent evidence; notice text/version; recipients/transfers; retention trigger/duration; rights/exception; accountable reviewer and effective date. Legal adviser should determine if additional national rules apply. No code may map “purpose” to a fixed basis simply by feature name.

## Cross-cutting sequence

For every flow establish: initiating feature and purpose, controller/processor role for that purpose, exact fields and provider, transport/storage encryption, location, provider retention, onward subprocessors, deletion/reconciliation API, logs, and evidence. Use synthetic fixtures for staging. A live `.env`, actual bucket metadata, provider contract or DNS record is not available from this source-only audit, so those remain explicit validation inputs.

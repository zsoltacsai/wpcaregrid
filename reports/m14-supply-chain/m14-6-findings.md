## Finding interpretation

F01 and F02 were High because they composed into an unreviewed-source-to-staging-root path. Both are now closed with effective GitHub ruleset readback and live staging sudo/helper evidence; neither is evidence of past compromise. M146-F10 is a confirmed fail-closed availability defect in the first deployment attempt. Baseline source was restored, workers/scheduler/HTTP recovered, and no candidate code switch occurred. M14.6 remains open until helper v2 is reviewed, installed through an explicit root/admin path, and exact-SHA staging validation succeeds.

No Critical finding was confirmed. No customer or production data was accessed. The full M14.7 attack campaign is not part of this audit.

## Finding interpretation

F01 and F02 are High because they compose into an unreviewed-source-to-staging-root path under concrete and independently read-back settings. They are **not** evidence of past compromise. The required verdict is D under the supplied severity rule. The minimum immediate containment recommendation is to stop staging release promotion until the authoritative-branch PR/check gate and staging deploy privilege boundary are corrected and independently verified. No containment was applied in this planning task.

No Critical finding was confirmed. No customer or production data was accessed. The full M14.7 attack campaign is not part of this audit.
